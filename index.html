Competition Categories

Three Elements of Flight

Choose your key discipline and showcase your mastery at the tournament's greatest height.

Category 01

Aerial Silks

A symbiosis of plasticity, breathtaking drops and flexibility at heights from 8 to 12 meters.

  • Solos and Duets
  • Kids, Amateurs, Pros
  • Rigging height up to 12 m
Choose Silks
Category 02

Aerial Hoop

Flawless geometry, dynamic spins and graceful balances inside the ring (Aerial Hoop / Lyra).

  • Single and double rigging
  • Spinning and static blocks
  • Artistic and sports categories
Choose Hoop
Category 03

Aerial Straps

A triumph of absolute physical strength, dynamic drops and balances on the straps (Aerial Straps).

  • Men's and Women's Solos
  • Strength elements in the Pro category
  • Duo performances
Choose Straps
